Privacy Policy for Arrow Traffic

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Arrow Traffic (“the Game”, app id com.zengothic.arrowtraffic on Android and iOS) is a single-player tap-to-escape arrow puzzle game published by Zengothic (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the Game and the services inside it handle, and what choices you have.

The short version: we do not run any server, we have no accounts, and we do not collect or receive any personal information about you. Your progress stays on your device. The Game does show advertisements, and the Google advertising service that delivers them collects data as described below. On iPhone the Game also offers one optional in-app purchase (VIP), processed entirely by Apple.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The Game has no sign-in, no user profiles, no chat, no leaderboards, no cloud save and no analytics of our own. We operate no servers, so no information about you is ever transmitted to us.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally, in the app's own private storage, so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device except through your phone's own backup feature (Android backup or iCloud backup), if you have that enabled in your device settings.

  • Which levels you have solved, with your star rating
  • How far you have unlocked
  • Your settings (sound, music)
  • Counters that pace the ads: how many levels you have finished, and how many optional ads you have chosen to watch today
  • Whether you own VIP, and how many VIP free skips you have used today

None of it identifies you. Uninstalling the Game removes it from your device. If you have your phone's backup switched on, a copy may remain in your Google account or iCloud until it is discarded; you can remove it yourself from your device's backup settings.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and is funded by advertising and an optional purchase. We use Google AdMob to show interstitial and rewarded ads. There are no banner ads. Full-screen ads appear only between levels, rate-limited. Rewarded ads are always optional — if a level has you stuck you may choose to watch one to move past it — and hints are free and unlimited, so every level can be finished without watching any ad.

To select and measure ads, Google collects and processes data from your device. Google acts as an independent data controller for this. Typically this includes:

Advertising ID (a resettable identifier)Ad selection, frequency capping, fraud prevention

On iPhone, the Game asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) before Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the identifier is not shared and you still see ads, just not personalised ones.

3a. In-app purchase (iPhone)

The Game offers one optional, one-time purchase, VIP: no forced ads, three free level skips a day, and a VIP badge on the home screen. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store under your Apple Account; we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple tells the Game only whether VIP was bought, so it can be switched on and restored with the Restore purchases button. Refunds are handled by Apple.

4. Your advertising choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can reset the ID or delete it entirely, which stops apps from receiving it. iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, where you can turn tracking off for the Game or for all apps.
  • Opt out of personalised ads. The same Android settings screen lets you turn off ad personalisation. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Where the law requires it, the Game shows Google's consent message the first time it runs, and your choice is passed to Google. To be asked again, clear the Game's data from Settings → Apps → Arrow Traffic → Storage on Android, or reinstall the Game on iPhone.

5. Children

Arrow Traffic is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will act on it — although, as described above, the Game itself collects nothing.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not hold any. The only third party that receives data from your device through the Game is Google, for the advertising purposes described in section 3 (and, on iPhone, Apple processes any purchase you make, as described in section 3a).

7. Data retention and deletion

Because we store nothing, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Game data held on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game (or clear its storage on Android). To have Google delete advertising data associated with your device, use the Android advertising ID controls in section 4 and Google's own privacy tools.

8. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private sandbox on your device, protected by the operating system's application isolation. Since no data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side store of your information to breach.

9. Changes to this policy

If the Game's data practices change, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing release notes.
